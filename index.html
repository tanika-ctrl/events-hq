Who we are

More than technical production. A team that's invested in getting it right.

We're a small and agile team of problem solvers. We're the team you call in when you want 100% confidence that no matter where your team are dialling in from or what ancient AV system the venue might have, you know we have you covered. With 20+ years of experience between us, we've learned that the difference between a good event and a forgettable one usually comes down to the details: sound that reaches the back row properly, a hybrid link that doesn't leave your remote audience feeling like an afterthought, a shot that's framed and lit the way it should be.

That's really where our specialisms come in. On the AV side, we're often brought in to sit alongside a larger event or production, handling the screens, sound and hybrid setup so an organiser or venue doesn't have to give it a second thought. It's the kind of work that goes unnoticed when it's done well, which is exactly the point. On the videography and editing side, we do the opposite — we make sure the moment gets noticed. Whether it's a product launch, a team milestone, or simply the everyday things your business is doing that are worth showing people, we've got the crew to capture it properly and edit it into something you'll actually want to share.

And if your event needs a view from above, that's covered too — through our sister team at Drones HQ, who we work alongside for aerial filming at a straightforward, affordable rate. No need to bring in a separate crew just to get a shot from the air.
